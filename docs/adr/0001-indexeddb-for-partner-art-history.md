# Use IndexedDB, not localStorage, for Partner Art History

Partner Art was previously persisted as a single item in `localStorage` as a base64 data URL. Adding Partner Art History means storing several images (capped at 10, MRU-evicted) instead of one, and there's a planned follow-up feature to persist the *entire* app session (all uploaded skins, store images, pack name/version) so a refresh or crash doesn't lose work in progress. `localStorage` is synchronous, string-only (forcing ~33% base64 overhead on binary images), and capped around 5-10MB total per origin — workable for one image, not for a whole session's worth of full-resolution skin textures. We chose to build the Partner Art History persistence on IndexedDB now, as a small generic key/value wrapper, so the later full-session-persistence feature extends this same layer instead of requiring a migration off localStorage.
